Use a few relevant labels to connect your video to a topic—not to disguise what it is about.

What are YouTube hashtags?

YouTube hashtags are words or phrases preceded by #, such as #IndoorGardening. They can appear in a title or description and link to pages of related content. Unlike video tags entered in settings, hashtags are visible to viewers.

They can help organize and connect related videos, but do not guarantee search rankings or views. The video, title and viewer experience still have to deliver on the topic.

Find relevant hashtag ideas

Write down the subject, the viewer’s goal and any meaningful category. A YouTube hashtag generator can suggest combinations; compare them with your actual video and discard anything vague or misleading. Search YouTube for a candidate to understand how people use it before adding it.

Use the keyword research guide to identify the question first, then pick labels that support it.

Choose and format hashtags

  • Keep each hashtag directly relevant to the content.
  • Use a small, readable set rather than filling the description.
  • Write multiword phrases without spaces, for example #BasilCare.
  • Avoid misleading names, unrelated trends and offensive or prohibited terms.

YouTube’s hashtag rules and display behavior can change. Excessive hashtags can be ignored, and unrelated or misleading metadata can create policy problems. Check current YouTube Help guidance before relying on a specific limit.

Examples by video category

These are illustrative ideas, not claims about popularity. Use them only when they match your video.

Common mistakes

Copying a trending label that has nothing to do with the video can disappoint viewers. So can using a long string of near-identical hashtags. A short descriptive title and helpful opening description are a better foundation; see our YouTube SEO guide for the full process.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are hashtags the same as tags?

No. Hashtags are visible, clickable labels; tags are a separate metadata field in video settings.

Will a popular hashtag guarantee views?

No. Relevance matters, and no hashtag guarantees distribution or ranking.

Where can I put hashtags?

YouTube supports them in video titles and descriptions. Use them only where they make sense to readers.
