About this resource

A little more clarity for creators figuring out YouTube search.

Why this site exists

Creator Fieldnotes helps YouTube creators understand search optimization, keyword research, video discoverability and practical SEO techniques through simple educational resources.

Our aim is to explain the work behind a good video: finding a real question, making something useful and describing it accurately. We favor practical examples over exaggerated promises.

Independent by design

This is an independent educational website. It is not affiliated with YouTube or SEO Studio Tools. References to those products are provided for context, not as a claim of partnership or endorsement.

Our guides are informational, not a guarantee of rankings, traffic, eligibility or earnings. Platform policies and tools change, so check the relevant provider’s current guidance before making an important decision.
