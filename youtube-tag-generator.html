Treat a tag generator as a brainstorming assistant—not a shortcut to a higher ranking.

What are YouTube tags?

Tags are descriptive terms added in a video’s settings. They are different from visible hashtags and from keywords: a keyword is a phrase someone might search for, while a tag is one optional metadata field where a creator can add context.

YouTube says tags play a minimal role in discovery for most videos, but they can help with common misspellings. Your title, thumbnail and description generally matter more. Tags alone do not guarantee rankings.

How to generate and select tags

  1. Describe the video’s main subject in a few plain words.
  2. Use a YouTube tag generator, or brainstorm related phrases and common spelling variations.
  3. Keep terms that accurately describe what is actually in the video.
  4. Remove near-duplicates, unrelated popular names and misleading claims.
  5. Recheck the title and description before spending more time on tags.

Start with the keyword research guide if you have not settled on the viewer’s question yet.

Long-tail terms and search intent

A broad topic like “basil” can mean recipes, gardening or plant problems. A longer phrase such as “how to prune basil indoors” points to a more specific need. Include a longer phrase as a tag only if the video actually addresses that question; the phrase is more valuable when the video itself answers it well.

A practical example

EXAMPLE VIDEO

Pruning basil for beginners

Possible tags: basil pruning, how to prune basil, indoor basil care, basil plant pruning.

Skip: unrelated plant names, a celebrity’s name or popular phrases that the video never addresses. The examples are suggestions, not measured search-volume data.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not paste a large list of tags into your description, use other creators’ names to attract unrelated searches, or treat every generator suggestion as accurate. A relevant few are more useful than a noisy collection. Review your complete video optimization workflow before publishing.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are keywords and tags the same thing?

No. Keywords are terms people may use to search; tags are one optional metadata field where relevant terms can be entered.

How many tags should I use?

There is no magic number. Use only relevant, useful tags within YouTube’s current field limits.

Can tags make a video rank first?

No. Tags have a limited role and cannot guarantee placement.
